Ok, next project. I built a RAG agent from scratch a while back, no frameworks, and I want to go a level deeper this time.

A tiny LLM inference server from scratch. The model, trained by hand, and the server that actually runs it fast under load.

The part I keep chewing on: naive generation recomputes the whole sequence every single token. A real server dodges that with a KV cache, batches requests together, schedules them, keeps the GPU busy.

I've leaned on this stuff for years and couldn't build a piece of it. That's the itch.

I'm Noah, an engineer at Hoodo Technologies. I build web and AI products for work, and on the side I do these from-scratch builds to actually understand the tools I lean on. Last one was the RAG agent, this one's the infra that serves models.

Building it all in public, one piece at a time, mistakes and all. Ends with a little demo you can throw traffic at and watch it sweat.
